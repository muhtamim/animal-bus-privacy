# Privacy Policy — Animal Bus: Color Match

**Effective date:** 2 October 2026
**App:** Animal Bus: Color Match (Android), application id `tech.nexevent.animalbus`
**Developer:** Next Event Technologies (https://nexevent.tech)
**Contact:** info@nexevent.tech

> This policy describes the app exactly as it is built. The current release
> contains **no advertising and no in-app purchases**. If a future version
> adds advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is
> published.

## 1. Who this game is for

Animal Bus: Color Match is a colour-and-shape matching game designed for
children aged about 6 to 8. Because children are the intended audience, the
app is built to work without collecting personal information.

## 2. Information the app does NOT collect

The app has no account system and never asks for a name, age, email address,
phone number, photo, or any other personal detail. It does not request the
camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or phone permissions. It does
not include analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, social features, chat, or
links to external websites inside the children's part of the app. It makes no
network connections.

## 3. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following **only on your device**, in its private app
storage:

- which levels have been completed and which level was played last;
- settings: music on/off, sound on/off, reduced motion, break reminder,
  language, chosen station theme;
- whether the short in-game guide has been shown.

This data never leaves the device, is excluded from Android cloud backup and
device-to-device transfer, and is removed when you uninstall the app. A
parent can also erase progress at any time from the **For grown-ups** area
inside the app (*Reset progress*).

## 4. Children's privacy

The app is intended for children and is designed so that no personal
information is collected from anyone. The **For grown-ups** area is protected
by a simple arithmetic question; this only keeps children away from settings
and is **not** a mechanism for obtaining parental consent, because no data is
collected that would require consent. If you believe a child has provided
personal information through this app in any way, contact
info@nexevent.tech and it will be deleted.

## 5. Permissions

The app declares **no Android permissions at all** (not even internet
access) and does not use the Android advertising identifier. You can verify
this on your device under Settings → Apps → Animal Bus → Permissions.

## 6. Third parties

No third-party services receive data from this app. The Google Play Store
itself collects installation and crash statistics under Google's own privacy
policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); the app does not add to that.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the
effective date revised. The current version is always available at
https://muhtamim.github.io/animal-bus-privacy/ and in the project
repository.
